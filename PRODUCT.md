# Product Context

## Register

Product.

## Users and purpose

Stremio Web helps desktop, mobile, and TV viewers browse their library, inspect titles, and start watching with minimal friction.

## Personality

Cinematic, focused, familiar, and calm. Preserve the existing Stremio identity and dark media-first interface.

## Anti-reference

Avoid the visual clutter, aggressive calls to action, and ad-heavy feel of generic streaming portals.

## Design principles

- Put playback and content first.
- Keep browsing context visible while watching.
- Reuse existing controls, spacing, typography, and responsive behavior.
- Preserve keyboard, gamepad, and clear focus-state support.

## Accessibility

Keep controls labeled, focus-visible, keyboard reachable, and legible over imagery. Respect reduced-motion preferences where motion is introduced.
